# Vrinda Store Annual Report

## Overview
This dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Vrinda Store's sales performance, customer demographics, and order trends. The insights are drawn from various dimensions, including time, regions, platforms, and customer attributes.

## Key Features
- **Orders vs Sales**: Monthly analysis of sales amount and order counts.
- **Top 5 Sales Regions**: Highlights top-performing states by sales volume.
- **Sales: Men vs Women**: Distribution of sales contribution by gender.
- **Orders by Channel**: Performance of sales channels like Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, etc.
- **Order Status**: Breakdown of delivered, canceled, refunded, and returned orders.
- **Age vs Gender Analysis**: Distribution of orders across age groups and genders.

---

## Insights
1. **Seasonality**:
   - Peak sales and orders occur between **March and July**.
   - Post-September sales and orders decline, indicating seasonal trends.

2. **Regional Performance**:
   - Top regions include **Maharashtra (2.99M)**, Karnataka (2.65M), and Uttar Pradesh (2.10M).
   - These regions contribute significantly to overall sales.

3. **Gender Contribution**:
   - **Women contribute 64% of total sales**, while men contribute 36%.
   - A targeted marketing strategy focusing on women could yield better results.

4. **Sales Channels**:
   - **Amazon (35%)**, Myntra (23%), and Flipkart (22%) are the top-performing platforms.
   - Strengthening partnerships with these platforms is essential.

5. **Order Fulfillment**:
   - **92.3% orders are delivered successfully**, with minimal cancellations or returns.

6. **Customer Demographics**:
   - Young women (18-34) and middle-aged women (35-54) are the largest contributors to orders.

---

## Recommendations
- Focus marketing campaigns during **March to July** to maximize returns.
- Prioritize high-performing regions like **Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh**.
- Target **young and middle-aged women shoppers** with tailored promotions.
- Strengthen partnerships with **Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart**.
- Maintain strong fulfillment processes to ensure high delivery success rates.
